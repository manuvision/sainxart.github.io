This distribution preserves the catalogue's ODbL attribution, although Guadeloupe specifically comes from IGN ADMIN EXPRESS. Data.gouv combines IGN geometry for France and DROM with other sources for COM.

Retrieved: 9 September 2026.
Source file timestamp in the official directory: 4 May 2026.
Feature selected: department code 971, Guadeloupe.
Source simplification: 100 metres; 14 polygons, all retained.

The geographical data in map.js and the accompanying guadeloupe-source.geojson are made available under ODbL 1.0. The source feature is included for reuse. The JavaScript rendering logic does not impose a licence change on the rest of the website.

Processing: local equirectangular projection centred on the source bounding box; longitude distance adjusted by cosine of centre latitude; uniform scale to 5 scene units wide; coordinates rounded to five decimal scene units; duplicate closing points removed. The original source feature has been retained without changing its geometry. No new geographic points were invented.

Interpretation: north up, original relative island positions. The 0.081-unit uniform extrusion is a graphic treatment, not measured terrain elevation. The map displays no pipe network, leak locations or water-loss distribution.
