Find your trail.

Walk with WASD or the arrow keys. Hold Shift to run. On touch screens, use the thumbstick.

Press E or A to examine something nearby. Otherwise, roll. Tap again during a roll to chain another. B or X swings the machete and cuts nearby herbs. Tap again to chain slashes, or press during a roll to slash as you land. Tikoon stays visible as a silhouette behind large trees and houses. Scroll or pinch to adjust your view.

Each slash or dodge roll costs one of your four energy points. One point recovers per second. Enemies fall after five slashes. Roll past their wind-up and counterattack. They cannot sprint. Rare red orbs found in cut herbs restore one health point and fly toward you when close.
