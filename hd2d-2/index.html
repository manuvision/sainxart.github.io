Find your trail.

Walk with WASD or the arrow keys. Hold Shift to run. On touch screens, use the thumbstick.

Press E or A to examine something nearby. Otherwise, roll. Tap again during a roll to chain another. B or X swings the machete and cuts nearby herbs. Tap again to chain slashes, or press during a roll to slash as you land. Tikoon stays visible as a silhouette when hidden behind scenery. Scroll or pinch to adjust your view.
