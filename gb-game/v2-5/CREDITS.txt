TIDELEAF COVE — v2.5

Environment graphics adapted from Pokémon FireRed / LeafGreen.
Original game graphics: Game Freak / Nintendo / The Pokémon Company.
Source tiles, metatile definitions and palettes: pret/pokefirered.
https://github.com/pret/pokefirered
Visual reference maps: Treasure Beach, Bond Bridge and Berry Forest,
archived by The Spriters Resource.
https://www.spriters-resource.com/game_boy_advance/pokemonfireredleafgreen/

Map arrangement and native Aseprite paintovers: ManuVision.
Revised foliage, timber frames, woven furnishings, terrain textures and
shared palette adaptations retain source underlays in the editable masters.
