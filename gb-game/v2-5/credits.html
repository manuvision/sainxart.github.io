Made from pixels.

Buildings, trees, flowers, shrubs and planters adapted from Serene Village — revamped v1.9 by LimeZu, used under CC BY 4.0.

ManuVision created the map arrangement, bridge, rooms, furniture and cuttable undergrowth, with new terrain transitions and palette edits to bring the environment together. The raccoon, machete and animations are our existing original pixel assets.

This revision replaces the earlier FireRed-derived environment. Older game versions have separate credits. LimeZu does not endorse this project.
